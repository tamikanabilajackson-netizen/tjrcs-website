## SECTION 1 — OPENING

I'm Tamika Jackson — a Recreation Professional, a founder, and someone who spent most of her life being told, in one way or another, what she needed to fix about herself.

Build & Launch exists because I decided to stop building programs around what's hard — and start building ones around what's already there.

## SECTION 2 — THE ORIGIN

I didn't plan to do this work.

I went to school to be a chemist. First at college for chemical engineering technology, then university in Toronto to complete my degree. I had a plan. I had a path.

While I was finishing that degree, I took a part-time job at a retirement home — just to pay the bills. When I graduated and the chemistry positions weren't coming, my manager asked me a question I wasn't expecting.

*"Would you like to be an activity assistant?"*

I said no. I told her I was a chemistry graduate. I had plans. She looked at me with the patience of someone who already knew the answer and said — *"But you don't have a job. And in the meantime, you can make extra money."*

So I said yes.

That moment changed everything. I found something in that role I hadn't expected to find anywhere — something intrinsically valuable. A way of working that took everything I loved about life, all of my ideas, all of who I am as a person, and wrapped it into one purpose.

It felt like it was made for me.

I've spent more than 15 years since then building programs that help people discover what they're capable of — applying therapeutic recreation principles across senior care, community settings, and now entrepreneurship coaching. And somewhere along the way, I stopped thinking of this work as something I fell into. I started thinking of it as something I was guided toward.

---

## SECTION 3 — THE NEURODIVERGENT THREAD

I'm also neurodivergent myself. And for most of my life, I didn't talk about that.

Not because I was ashamed of it — but because the conversation around neurodivergence, in my experience, almost always went the same direction. Toward what was hard. Toward the challenges, the workarounds, the techniques for managing the things that didn't come easily.

I spent years getting very good at exactly that — managing. Developing coping strategies. Compensating. Making sure nobody could see the places where things were harder for me than they looked.

What I didn't do — what almost nobody encouraged me to do — was pay the same attention to my strengths. The things that came more naturally. The interests that lit something up. The parts of me that were already ready to grow. Those didn't get the same room.

And then AI came into my life. And something shifted.

For the first time, I had a tool that could meet me where I actually was. I could speak my ideas — even when they weren't perfectly organized, even when the words didn't come out in the order I meant them — and have them shaped into something the world could see. My thoughts. My dreams. My voice. Without having to first pass them through every workaround I'd spent years building.

It didn't fix anything. It removed a barrier I'd been quietly working around my whole life.

And when I felt what that was like — I knew immediately who else needed to feel it.

---

## SECTION 4 — WHY BUILD & LAUNCH

I built Build & Launch for people who are still figuring out where their strengths fit — whatever that looks like for them.

For some, that's a young adult who's spent years having their challenges get all the attention — the IEPs, the accommodations, the strategies — while their strengths, interests, and natural abilities got noted and quietly set aside in favour of what needs to be managed.

For others, it's a beginner entrepreneur standing at the edge of an idea, unsure how to turn it into a brand, a LinkedIn presence, or a business that actually reflects who they are — held back not by a lack of ability, but by never having had the tools shaped around them specifically.

Different starting points. Same underlying belief: that the fastest way to build something real is to start with what's already there, not what's missing.

The business is the framework. The career exploration is the vehicle. But what Build & Launch is really about — underneath all of it — is giving someone the experience of being seen for what they're capable of, not just what they're working on.

That's the thing I had to find my own way to. I wanted to build the door — and I built it wide enough for more than one kind of person to walk through.

---

## SECTION 5 — HOW SHE WORKS

Every person who comes into a Build & Launch program gets something genuinely built around them.

Not adapted from a generic template. Not modified to accommodate differences. Built — from the first session — around their strengths, their communication style, their goals, and their pace.

Before every session, I review what I know about each person and adjust accordingly. The structure stays consistent — because predictability matters, and I know firsthand what session-entry anxiety feels like — but the content is entirely theirs.

I'm not here to tell anyone what their future should look like. I'm here to help them figure out what fits — and then build the skills and confidence to move toward it.

That's what therapeutic recreation principles, applied well, actually look like in practice.
